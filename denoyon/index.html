Print constraints

All text outlined. Thinnest line 0.4 mm. Spot inks: ink, cream, brass metallic, oxblood. Dark garments print cream as the underbase plate; brass is flashed last.

Brand rules held

No discount codes, countdowns or “only 2 left”. Sale is Archive. Promotions are services: alteration, fitting days, gift wrapping.

Photography

None exists yet. Every image well is a captioned placeholder naming the shot it needs, per the system.
