Size guide · Bias tee

Garment measurements, laid flat. Cut on the bias, so the tee falls closer at the hip than the chest measure suggests.

SizeA · ChestB · LengthC · ShoulderD · HemFR / IT / UK
  1. Chest. Pit to pit, laid flat, doubled.
  2. Length. Highest shoulder point to hem, down the centre back.
  3. Shoulder. Seam to seam across the back.
  4. Hem. Side to side at the hem, doubled. The bias adds 2 cm of drape.

Between sizes, take the smaller. Alterations to hem length are complimentary.
