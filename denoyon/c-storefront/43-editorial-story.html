Atelier · Story 03

The 1974 book.

Paris, 8e · 6 min

A pattern found in a Lyon attic.

Thirty-one pattern books, 1948 to 1979, bought from the estate of a couturier who cut for private clients. The 1974 book holds a single t-shirt pattern, drafted on the bias.

It was never produced. The atelier cut it for the first time in 2026.

Pattern book open on the cutting table · overhead · 4:3

“The cloth decides where it falls. The cutter only decides where it starts.”
Head cutter, the atelier

Why the bias.

Cut at 45° to the grain, jersey drapes instead of hanging. It uses a third more cloth, and every edge has to be finished by hand.
